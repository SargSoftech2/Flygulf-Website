ACADEMY OVERVIEW

FlyGulf International
Career Academy !

FlyGulf International Career Academy is a leading healthcare education and certification institute dedicated to preparing nurses, doctors, pharmacists, physiotherapists, and allied healthcare professionals for successful international careers. We provide comprehensive coaching for DHA, MOH, HAAD, NCLEX, PROMETRIC, ACLS, BLS, PALS, OET, and IELTS examinations. Our training programs are designed to combine theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and exam-focused preparation to help students achieve licensing success. Through expert mentorship, modern training methodologies, and career guidance, we support healthcare professionals in building rewarding careers across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, the United Kingdom, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, and other global healthcare destinations.
